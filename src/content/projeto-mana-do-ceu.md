**Maná do Céu** began as a simple idea: give daily devotional messages a home online, where more people could find and read them.

My father, Pastor Eli Vilela, had been sharing short messages for years through text messages and social networks. I wanted to turn that personal habit into a website that made the messages easier to discover and share.

My aunt, his sister, was already receiving the messages each day. She called them *Maná*—manna, the provision that sustained the people of Israel in the wilderness. That became the project's name.

## A website and a podcast

When I wrote this post in 2016, the project published a new devotional on its website every day. We also created a podcast so people could listen on their phones.

The original project links were:

- [Maná do Céu website](http://www.manadoceu.com/)
- [Podcast listing on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/br/podcast/mana-do-ceu-devocionais-diarios/id1115805667)
- [RSS feed](http://feeds.feedburner.com/manadoceu)

These are historical links from the launch; the archive does not establish whether the website or feed is still maintained.

The purpose was straightforward: make a short moment of reflection accessible wherever people were, through the channels they already used.
